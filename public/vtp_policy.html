Privacy Policy for Kushal CG

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Kushal CG. This application has been developed for attendance management of Vocational Trainers. The app helps trainers mark attendance with location verification to confirm whether they are present at the assigned school. It also supports face verification, leave applications, monthly attendance reports, and on-duty attendance records.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect the information obtained through the app. By using this app, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects and processes the following information only for attendance and related official purposes:

  • Location Data: To verify that the Vocational Trainer is present at the assigned school while marking attendance.
  • Camera Access: To capture or verify the trainer's face during registration and attendance marking.
  • Face Image: During registration, the trainer may upload a face image. This image is used to verify that the same person is marking attendance later.
  • Attendance Records: To store check-in, check-out, date, time, school, on-duty, and attendance status information.
  • Leave Information: To process leave applications submitted by the trainer.
  • Internet Access: To securely upload and sync attendance, leave, face verification, and report data with the system.

No additional personal data such as contacts, messages, or personal files is collected.

2. How We Use the Information

Collected information is used only to:

  • Confirm Vocational Trainer presence at the assigned school.
  • Verify that attendance is being marked by the registered trainer using face verification.
  • Maintain daily attendance, on-duty attendance, leave requests, and monthly attendance reports.
  • Upload attendance and verification data securely for official review and record keeping.

We do not sell, share, or distribute user data to unauthorized third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All collected data, including location, attendance records, leave details, and face images used for verification, is securely transmitted to our servers.
  • Security measures are applied to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
  • Face images are used only for identity verification related to attendance and are not used for any unrelated purpose.

4. Permissions Used

The App requires the following permissions:

  • Camera: To capture or verify the trainer's face for registration and attendance verification.
  • Location (GPS and Network): To verify trainer presence at the assigned school while marking attendance.
  • Internet: To upload and sync attendance records, face verification data, leave applications, and monthly reports securely.

Permissions are used strictly for their stated purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share user data with external parties, except when required by law or authorized by the managing organization.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for authorized Vocational Trainers and official users only and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in this document and, where appropriate, communicated through the App.
